01 — Scope

I am proposing to design and build the digital system for Samara Residence Hotel: the public website, online booking, and the internal software that runs reservations and the dashboard.

The brand covers three lodges — Emburara, Chimpundu, and a third property still to be named. The architecture is built for three from day one, so the third lodge is a configuration, not a rebuild.

This is a product build. Luxury look on the front. Clean operations on the back.

04b — How the website looks

Page by page, as a guest sees it.

The public site is dark: ink background, gold lines, ivory type, the white Samara mark, the three hill lines. Not a booking.com clone. One header on every public page. Mobile first — the same pages stack on a phone.

Chrome on every public page

Header

Left: Samara logo. Center / right: Home · Lodges · Rooms · Book · Contact · Support. Far right: Sign in / Sign up. After a guest logs in, those two become their first name + a small menu (My account, Sign out). Admin never appears in this header.

Look

Type: Cinzel for labels, Cormorant Garamond for titles, Outfit for body. Gold on dark. Lodge cards use the hill mark, not stock photography. Footer: lodge names, email, phone / WhatsApp if you provide them, and a short line for Uganda.

Home

Full-viewport opening: white logo, one line for the three lodges, a Book now button. Scroll: three lodge cards (Emburara, Chimpundu, Lodge 03) — name, short text, link to that lodge. Then a short booking strip (dates + lodge). Footer. No carousel of stock rooms. Slow, editorial, not a busy hotel template.

Lodges index

A page that lists all three properties as large cards. Each card: lodge name, location line (when you have it), “View lodge”. Same layout so Lodge 03 slots in when named.

Lodge page

One URL per property. Top: lodge name + short description. Then room types as a list or cards — name, occupancy, rate, Check dates. Amenities only as a text block you confirm. Location line or map when you have an address. Same structure on Emburara, Chimpundu, and Lodge 03.

Rooms

Filter by lodge at the top. Cards below: room name, occupancy, rate, availability for selected dates. Click a card → room detail (description, rate rules) and a Book button that carries lodge + room into the booking flow.

Amenities

A simple page or a section on each lodge. Placeholder until you list what is actually offered. Nothing invented (no spa / restaurant copy unless you confirm it).

Booking

Stepper, not an OTA grid. 1 Lodge → 2 Dates + guests → 3 Room (only what is free) → 4 Guest details + pay. Currencies: UGX default, USD / EUR / GBP. Pay: card and mobile money. Success: reservation ID (e.g. SMR-1842) and “We sent this to your email.” If they have an account, the stay is already under My reservations.

My reservation

If logged in: their stays. If not: a lookup — email + reservation ID — then the same stay card (lodge, dates, status). They cannot see anyone else’s booking.

Contact

Centered form on the dark page: name, email, lodge (optional dropdown), message. Phone / WhatsApp shown if you want them public. Submit → “Message sent.” The row appears in the admin Contact inbox.

Support

For an existing stay. Fields: email, reservation ID, subject, message. Mismatch of email + ID is rejected. Match opens a ticket in the dashboard. Logged-in guests can skip to My account → Support with the ID already filled.

Sign up / Sign in

Two short screens, same gold form language. Sign up: name, email, password, confirm. Sign in: email, password, forgot password. After either, they land in My account on the public site — never /admin.

06 — Accounts

Signup, login, and who sees what.

Two separate logins. A guest email never opens the admin dashboard. An admin email never opens a guest account. Every stay has a reservation ID on both sides.

When an admin logs in

Staff open /admin — a separate URL, not the public Sign in. They use the email you assigned (owner, lodge admin, or staff). After login they stay inside the dashboard. The public website header is gone. They see a left sidebar and a working page.

Login screen

Centered form on the dark admin theme: email, password, Enter dashboard. Forgot password sends a reset to that admin email. A guest email is refused here. After a correct login they land on Overview.

Shell

Left sidebar (all pages listed below). Top bar: logged-in name, role (Owner / Lodge admin / Staff), lodge switcher if they have more than one, Sign out. Lodge admin only sees their lodge in every list. Owner sees Emburara, Chimpundu, and Lodge 03.

Overview

Today’s numbers: occupancy, arrivals, departures, open support tickets, unread contact messages. Occupancy bars for each lodge. A short table of today’s arrivals (reservation ID, guest, lodge, room). Each card / row links into the matching module.

Reservations

Table of all bookings. Columns: reservation ID, guest name, email, lodge, room, dates, status, amount. Search by ID or email. Filter by lodge and status. Click a row → full stay: payment, extras, notes, support tickets on that ID. Actions: confirm, edit dates, cancel, add a manual booking.

Calendar

Month view per lodge. Occupied / vacant / blocked nights. Click a day → arrivals and departures for that date.

Rooms

Room list by lodge. Status: vacant, occupied, due out, blocked. Housekeeping list. Maintenance note on a room. Create or edit a room type (name, rate, occupancy) — this updates the website.

Guests

Every customer account: name, email, reservation IDs, stay history, notes. Admin can open a guest and see only that person’s bookings — not used as a public profile.

Contact inbox

Messages from the contact page. Unread / replied. Name, email, lodge, date, body. Reply from here; it goes to the guest email.

Support

Tickets from the support page. Each ticket shows email, reservation ID, subject, thread. Status: open, in progress, closed. Admin replies; guest sees the reply under My account → Support.

Reports

Occupancy and revenue by lodge and by date range. Booking source: website, phone, walk-in. Export if needed. Lodge admin sees only their lodge. Owner sees all three.

Website content

Edit lodge text, room names, rates, amenities. Changes show on the public site. No code required.

Team

Admin users, each tied to one email. Roles: owner (all lodges), lodge admin (one lodge), staff (reservations + rooms only). Add / disable a user. Password reset to that email.

Settings

Lodge names, currency (UGX default), payment methods, contact details shown on the site, notification emails. Owner only. Staff do not see this page.

When a customer signs up

From the public header: Sign up. This creates a guest account only. They cannot choose a staff role. After submit they are signed in as a customer and stay on the public site.

Pages they can open

Sign in screen

Email + password. Forgot password → reset link to that email. An admin email on this form is blocked or sent to /admin — they do not enter a guest account. After login: My account.

Header while logged in

Logo and public links stay. Sign in / Sign up are replaced by their name. Menu: My account, My reservations, Support, Profile, Sign out. No Dashboard link.

My account / Reservations

Left: account menu. Right: their reservation IDs only — lodge, room, dates, status (confirmed, upcoming, past, cancelled). Click a row → stay detail. Empty state if they have none.

Stay detail

Reservation ID, lodge, room, dates, guest count, amount paid, extras. Status. Button: Contact support about this stay (opens a ticket with this ID). They cannot edit another person’s stay or see the admin notes field.

My support

Their tickets only. List: subject, reservation ID, status, last reply. New ticket: email locked to their account + a reservation ID they own. They see admin replies in the thread.

Profile

Edit name, phone, password. Email stays the account key (change only with a verification mail). Sign out returns them to the public header (Sign in / Sign up again).

Public site still works

They can still open Home, lodges, rooms, book another stay, contact. Booking is prefilled. They do not see Overview, Calendar, Rooms board, Guests, Team, Reports, or anyone else’s reservations.
